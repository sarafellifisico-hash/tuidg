The Alpha and Epsilon Structural Levels

The Alpha Level begins the six-level hierarchy with Merons and Meronic aggregates as its basic geometric and functional constituents.

The Alpha and Epsilon Structural Levels

Figure x — Merons, the constituent elements of the Alpha Level

Alpha Level, the First Structural Level

The Alpha Level is formed by all the infinitely many existing Merons (atoms) acting as basic functional units distributed throughout the spatially infinite universe—that is, the infinite Omega Dimension.

The functional elements falling within the Alpha Level's dimensional range span from the scale of the "smallest" and simplest Meron (the Metalpha)—including molecules and molecular aggregates the size of dust grains—up to the scale of the largest existing stars. All these elements serve as basic functional units; that is, each element acts as an autonomous organism immersed in a sea of organisms of the same nature.

All bodies within this dimensional range are, so to speak, merely small or large Meronic aggregates, whether or not they possess a system of other orbiting aggregates (bodies). They are all constituted of Merons acting as basic building blocks, and all belong to the same dimensional range.
