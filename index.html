01 / THE PREMISE

One universe.
No material substance.

TUIDG begins with a radical proposition: reality is not built from indivisible corpuscles. It is an absolute void — the Omega Dimension — structured by immaterial geometric components and their logical, static relationships.

Central idea
“Apparent matter” is the effect of geometry interacting across infinite structural dimensions.

02 / LOGICAL FOUNDATION

Four principles restrict what reality can be.

Not assumptions about particles, but tests of logical possibility.

01

Void–Matter Indistinction

No absolute boundary can distinguish being from non-being.

02

Indefinite Fragmentation

Every supposedly minimal element implies an internal intermediate element.

03

Constructive Interaction

Separated elements cannot interact without a directly connected third element.

04

Indefinite Containment

Every proposed maximum volume implies a greater containing volume.
