Rotating Interaction

Bearing in mind that the fundamental elements of the universe are not "fundamental particles" of matter, but are merely geometric elements of toroidal form called Compalphas, with their structure formed by 6 types of Interaction Circles, which are the faithful representation of the "force fields" in space, then what could be that which quantum physicists call the "color field," the "electric field," the "magnetic field," and the gravitational field?

Now, if we were speaking in conventional physical terms, we could say that the six types of Interaction Circles of the Compalphas would be like the (imaginary) "lines of force" of six types of "force fields" or, more precisely, that they would be the "lines of force" of six types of intrinsic and concentric "magnetic fields" or even six types of intrinsic "strong fields," generated by a single type of "fundamental particle" without "mass." Thus, the Interaction Circles of the Compalphas would be the "lines of force" of all the "force fields" of nature.

However, as there are no "fundamental particles without mass," since this proposition is simply contradictory, and what exists are geometric elements without substantiality and without real movement, then the Interaction Circles of the Compalphas may be understood as the geometric structures responsible for the six types of "action at a distance" that form all types of "substances" in the universe. As the Compalphas are interconnected in infinite number, the Interaction Circles, by virtue of the rule of the Rotating Interaction, may also be understood as the elements that form the "continuous and infinite geometric mesh" that is the Geometric Continuum of TUIDG.

These Interaction Circles being the sole agents responsible for the characteristics of "attraction" and "repulsion" between the elements of Apparent Matter — such as those that exist between the "electric," "magnetic," and gravitational "fields" — as well as for the formation, stabilization, and rupture of this geometric mechanism through the action of the Cyclic Interaction. The interactive action of these Interaction Circles, in the case of an "attractive interaction," occurs when the rotation of a given type of Interaction Circle of any Compalpha is in "rotational synchronism" with the rotation of another Interaction Circle of the same type belonging to any other Compalpha. Whereas a "repulsive interaction" between Compalphas occurs when the rotation of a given type of Interaction Circle is in "rotational asynchronism" with a similar Interaction Circle of any other Compalpha.

The Compalphas interact to form the Merons through the mechanism of the Rotating Interaction, by means of the connection of their Interaction Circles and with the participation of the Cyclic Interaction. This Rotating Interaction manifests itself in two "antagonistic" modes, which are the Synchronous Rotation Mode (Attractor Spin) and the Asynchronous Rotation Mode (Repulsor Spin). The Synchronous Rotation Mode, according to each type of Interaction Circle, unfolds into three (3) sub-modes, which are: Meronic Synchronous Rotation, Molecular Synchronous Rotation, and Exomolecular Synchronous Rotation; while the Repulsor Spin Mode, according to the types of circles, also unfolds into three sub-modes, which are: Intrameronic Asynchronous Rotation, Intramolecular Asynchronous Rotation, and Intraexomolecular Asynchronous Rotation.

These qualities are merely fundamentally apparent existences, for what exists are infinite sets of three types of Geometric Continuums, whose constitutive elements are the Compalphas, which are based upon an arbitrary Structural Dimension, and within which the mechanisms of the Rotating Interaction and the Cyclic Interaction take place. Such geometric mechanisms do not possess real movement, but rather an Apparent Movement that is perceived by us due to the relations among the infinite static geometric superpositions of these structures, and it is this Apparent Movement that generates the notion or impression of time and of the various forms of energies and forces of the universe. In Apparent Movement, the Compalphas merely appear to execute movements of every nature, and thus are capable of interacting through modes other than those of the Rotating Interaction and the Cyclic Interaction. And it is these "secondary interactions" which, by acting at various higher levels, may even be called "forces," for they are part of the world that we can perceive and of which we are part.

Physicists believe that there exists a type of intrinsically "positive" fundamental particle and another that is "negative," as well as a type that is intrinsically "neutral," but, according to the logic of the geometric structures of TUIDG, all these supposed "distinct interactions" are, in truth, merely the result of the mode of rotation of the various types of Interaction Circles of the Compalphas. Thus, when the mechanism of the rotations of the Interaction Circles of two (or more) Compalphas is "synchronous," the process of interaction between them is "attractive," and when the rotations are "asynchronous," the interactive process is "repulsive." It is from the equilibrium between these two processes that the characteristic of neutrality arises among the Compalphas, and hence among the Merons.

If we were to speak of these fundamental interactions in conventional terms, from particle physics, then we could say that any two "particles" attract one another when the vectors of their "magnetic fields" — which would be "intrinsic magnetic fields" — are complementary; that is, when the "dipoles" of the two (or more) "particles" are configured in space such that the "north pole" of one interacts with the "south pole" of the other, exactly as occurs in the interaction between two magnets. Only in this case, we would be speaking of nanometric magnets, the smallest possible magnets. And it is precisely this "magnetic" aspect that the Compalphas of TUIDG possess.
