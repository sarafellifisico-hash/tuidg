The Absolute Empty Space of the Universe

Absolute Empty Space is presented as the infinite and dimensionless container of all immaterial geometric structures.

Absolute Empty Space

Considering the philosophical and physical conceptions regarding the element of space, it is inevitable that we must encounter the classic paradoxes arising from its erroneous qualitative and quantitative characteristics, such that it appears there are countless distinct conceptions of space. However, if we deeply analyze them all, we will see that there are only two distinct conceptions. The first is that space truly exists as something absolutely empty and distinct from the supposed materiality of forms; this is classical space, Newtonian space.

The Attributes of Absolute Empty Space

The thesis is as follows: Space is immaterial, dimensionless, amorphous, motionless, and atemporal. Therefore, it cannot be said that the Absolute Empty Space described by TUGIA can possess reality or materiality, that it can be fractionated or extended infinitely, that it has this or that form, that it could have been generated, that it can inflate or collapse, or that it possesses numerous dimensions of its own, since these characteristics can only be attributed to the elements and objects contained and distributed within it—that is, to the elements formed by the Immaterial Geometry that constitutes the Apparent Matter described in this theory.

According to the logic in which TUGIA is structured, the shortest distance between two points in Absolute Empty Space is, undoubtedly, given by a straight line segment; this is an axiomatic statement. Regarding the physical impossibility of two bodies connecting via a "straight path" (a line) or the fact that their distance measurements do not correspond to measurements made in Absolute Empty Space, such as lengths or congruence angles of a triangle, this is not due to the properties of space itself, but to physical constraints of the same constitutive nature in which the bodies are immersed, due to the types of "surfaces" upon which these geometric objects are "drawn."

Thus, on the spherical surface of the Earth, the shortest distance between two points A and B, determined by the barrier of its physical constitution (the ground), is indeed a curve. The trigonometric relations of the geometry used to measure distances on the Earth's surface do indeed take its curvature into account; however, we must distinguish that this does not concern the curvature of the Absolute Empty Space itself that contains the points along with the Earth's surface, but rather the "drawings" made on a spherical surface. Nevertheless, this Absolute Empty Space, with infinite dimension, is by no means something devoid of characteristics, for we shall see that it possesses certain attributes defined by the following five postulates:

I. Absolute Empty Space is Immaterial; it possesses no materiality, being the absolute absence of everything we understand as reality; it is, strictly, an Absolute Empty Space.

II. Absolute Empty Space is Dimensionless; for to establish any quantified volume for it, it would be necessary for it to be contained by something external to itself, with the same necessary characteristic of containment, in a process of containment that is necessarily indefinite.

III. Absolute Empty Space is Amorphous; for an absolutely dimensionless void makes the generation of a final form impossible, since every notion of form derives from the notion of volume, which derives from quantification.

IV. Absolute Empty Space is Motionless; for being Immaterial, Dimensionless, and Amorphous, there is nothing to move, nor anywhere to move to, since motion is the product of the infinite change of forms from one place to another in space. It is, therefore, an attribute of something quantified that changes position in space.

V. Absolute Empty Space is Atemporal; time is merely the product of the relationship between different intensities of Apparent Motion, and this is a consequence of the relationship between the infinite positions of forms throughout space. Thus, the Absolute Space itself, which has neither form nor motion, is atemporal, for it did not emerge, evolve, or will it disappear throughout a time of its own.

The characteristics of the Absolute Space of TUGIA are quite different from the descriptions of space in conventional physics, which possess shapes, curvatures, and delimited volumes, expansion speeds over time, and are subject to deformations—in other words, true mythological narratives. It is in this Absolute Space, in this true space described by TUGIA, that absolutely every type of geometric form, with infinite dimensions of its own, is distributed along Three Directions and Three Absolute Senses, perpendicular to one another—that is, along the Six Orientations of Absolute Space.

Light Refraction and the Supposed Curvature of Space

The thesis upheld by TUGIA is as follows: The deviation observed in light passing near celestial bodies, such as planets, stars, or galaxies, is not caused by the supposed "curvature" of the supposed "spacetime" around these bodies, as the theory of relativity asserts. Rather, the deviation of light is due to simple refraction of light rays, resulting from the density gradient formed by the gases that constitute the various atmospheric layers surrounding them. The primary observational phenomenon that corroborates the general theory of relativity, if not the only one, is the fact that light is deflected when passing near a cosmic object, such as a star. In 1919, a similar observational experiment was conducted in the city of Sobral, Ceará, Brazil, aiming to demonstrate that space could be influenced by the presence of matter to the extent of "curving," thereby corroborating the idea that space can also expand (Big Bang) or contract (Big Crunch).

However, according to the logic of the immutable Absolute Empty Space of TUGIA, this optical phenomenon, supposedly due to the effect produced by the sun's mass on the space around it, was nothing more than the observation that the "rays" of light from distant stars that "passed" near the sun's surface were, in fact, deflected by the commonplace phenomenon of light refraction caused by the difference in density between the vacuum of space and the "atmosphere" of the sun. Were it not for the fact that astronomers found a real deviation of light, relativity would have been a complete failure. But the team of physicists at the time already knew that a deviation would be found, as they already knew that stars possess atmospheres, that all stars are immersed in a "bubble of gas" with a pressure gradient in relation to the space surrounding them; the rest of the story was merely a great "scientific marketing" maneuver—a device required by the scenario of theoretical difficulty in the field of gravitation in physics during those years, yet one that persists to this day because there is nothing better.

Nevertheless, according to the propositions of TUGIA, space, in itself, cannot undergo any type of influence originating from the "mass" of bodies, just as it cannot influence them in any way, much less as described by this "quantum-relativistic" conjuncture. The space that exists around the immeasurable "mass" of quasars is the same as that which exists around the miserable supposed photons of light—that is, it is nothingness itself, such that it makes no sense for sinuous trajectories of supposed "light corpuscles" (a quantum concept) to exist through a supposed "curved space" (a relativistic concept). According to the logical propositions of TUGIA regarding what space and gravity between bodies truly are, the deviation of light is not caused by any "curvature" of any "spacetime," but is caused by simple refraction produced by the gases and dust that compose all types of orbital systems (such as the solar system) in the universe. It is solely deviations caused by light refractions resulting from the different refractive indices provided by the "clouds" of gaseous elements that exist around bodies—primarily the residual hydrogen gas from the clouds that formed them, which permeates all bodies in the universe.

Thus, we can predict that deviations in the trajectory of starlight can be produced by both massive objects, such as stars or galaxies, and by less massive objects, such as planets and satellites (moons), provided that such bodies possess some atmosphere capable of generating a perceptible density difference between the vacuum and their layers closest to the surface—that is, provided that there is a "gradient" of pressure and density around these celestial bodies. And they always possess this to some degree. We can prove this thesis by conducting an experiment similar to that performed in Brazil in 1919, using as a "gravitational lens" the planet Mercury or our Moon, since both possess practically no atmosphere when compared to the sun or the Earth. However, as Mercury is composed basically of metals (iron in greater proportion), it would be the best candidate, given that its density of 5.43 g/cm³ is the second highest in the solar system, second only to Earth’s, and thus, more massive and, therefore, more "gravitic" than the Moon.

Consequently, according to this possible experiment, when the system formed by the Sun, Mercury, and the observer—which could be the SOHO space telescope—is appropriately positioned in space, we will not measure a deviation in the trajectory of the light coming from the sun's surface that is proportional to what is expected by the calculations of the theory of relativity. A deviation would be easily denoted by the nuances of the spots and specific patterns on the solar surface. As a complement to the proof, we could use the planet Venus, which possesses a thick atmosphere and for which, by reason of this, we could expect deviations or distortions to be found in the trajectory of light, whereas we would not find them in Mercury. Therefore, according to the propositions of TUGIA, there is no supposed "curvature" of a supposed "spacetime" around any celestial body, for space is nothingness itself; furthermore, there is no need for "dark matter" or "dark energy" to complete the cohesive forces between the structures of galaxies and those formed by them, but only the phenomenon of classical refraction, occasioned by the difference in density of the gaseous layers formed by the same hydrogen that constitutes all stars and which residually surrounds all bodies in the universe.
