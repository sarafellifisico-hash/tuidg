Iota Level, the Third Structural Level

The Iota Level is the level comprising all the infinitely many existing galaxies acting as basic functional units distributed throughout the universe as a whole—that is, throughout the infinite Omega Dimension.

The functional structures falling within the Iota Level Dimensional Range span sizes ranging from protogalaxies to fully formed galaxies—from the smallest to the largest in existence. Each acts as a basic functional unit—akin to an autonomous and, to a certain extent, independent microorganism—capable of forming all types of existing galactic clusters and superclusters, as these larger structures utilize galaxies as their basic building blocks.

All functional elements within this Dimensional Range are, in fact, composed of galaxies serving as basic building blocks.

Figure x — Galactic superclusters, the constituent elements of the Nu Level

The Nu Level, the Fourth Structural Level

The Nu Level consists of the infinite set of structures—what we understand as galactic superclusters—acting as basic functional units distributed throughout the infinite space of the universe, that is, the infinite Omega Dimension.

The functional elements falling within this Dimensional Band are structures that, given their unmistakable forms, can be termed Cosmic Neurons. As a whole, this structure resembles wisps of smoke or fluttering, interconnected membranes; denser regions form—so to speak—nuclei resembling a tissue of interconnected cells, where each cell appears akin to a nerve cell in the human brain.

This gigantic assemblage of galactic superclusters naturally forms other structures even larger than themselves. Given the inevitable biological context of these cosmic structures, these subsequent formations can only be named the Cosmic Cortex (the Body of Cosmic Neurons), since their constituents are known as Cosmic Neurons. Thus, all these infinite Cosmic Neurons are formed, above all, by galaxies acting as basic functional units—as fundamental building blocks.
