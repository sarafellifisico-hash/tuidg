Rho Level, the Fifth Structural Level

The Rho Level is formed by the totality of Cosmic Neurons acting as basic functional elements. The Rho Level cannot be observed directly; it can only be generated by aggregating data acquired and processed by supercomputers utilizing complex mathematical models.

The overall appearance of this structure resembles the outer regions of the (human) brain—where the number of cell nuclei exceeds that of the deeper regions—known as the Cerebral Cortex; hence the name given to this cosmic structure: the Cosmic Cortex.

Venturing into the realm of pure speculation, one might surmise that the infinite set of Cosmic Cortices could well constitute what might be termed the Cosmic Mind.

Thus, this vast, virtually infinite expanse known as the Cosmic Cortex is composed primarily of Cosmic Neurons serving as basic functional units—as fundamental building blocks.

Omega Level, the Sixth Structural Level

The Omega Level is formed by the aggregate of all structures from the previous level—or everything observable and calculable by current technological instruments; it is the universe itself in its full structural form.

Given the premise that any and all types of individual functional elements or structures, when combined or interconnected, form other types of structures, it stands to reason that the structures originating from the Rho Level are also capable of constituting other, higher-order structures.

And this immediately higher structure is the ultimate structure—the Omega Level itself, the great cosmic mind, whose functional elements are the parts or areas of the various Cosmic Cortices. Even if our observational technology were to become vastly superior to what it is today, what we would see would still be merely parts of that same structure.

Extrapolating this relationship between cosmic structure and biological structure, we might surmise that beyond this Cosmic Mind, there could exist a head with a body and limbs—whether humanoid in form or otherwise—along with other similar beings among a multitude of coexisting entities; beings who have spouses and children, social lives, societies, and worlds of their own, complete with their own stars, galaxies, and everything else.

The Coexisting Hexa-levels of Structural Organization

It is highly probable that the previous extrapolation is not merely an extrapolation, but rather one possible reality among infinite others. According to TUIDG, the universe contains more than just the six levels of organization—or Hexa-levels of Structural Organization—since the universe's functional structures (Merons, planets, stars, etc.) are fundamentally formed by Compalphas: the basic geometric elements that constitute Geometric Continua, Hypercontinua, and Ultracontinua (as discussed in Chapter IV).

Since the dimension of these basic elements is essentially arbitrary—chosen from infinite possibilities—any such dimension could give rise to an entire structural hierarchy. This hierarchy, in turn, would necessarily fall within the same six higher levels previously described as the Hexa-levels of Structural Organization. Because the functional processes or mechanisms of Compalphas, Merons, molecules, and the aggregates of this "apparent matter" always obey the two logical functional mechanisms—Rotating Interaction and Cyclic Interaction—regardless of their specific dimensions, functional similarities inevitably exist across all structures derived from them.

Thus, for every arbitrary base dimension of Geometric Continua, Hypercontinua, and Ultracontinua, there exists a corresponding set of six Levels of Organization for the resulting structures; this is because no other type of functional mechanism can exist that is fundamentally different from the Rotating and Cyclic Interaction mechanisms. Consequently, each of these Levels of Organization is functionally identical, with variations occurring perhaps only in the nuances of the shapes of galaxies, clusters, superclusters, and all other higher-order structures. Thus, in accordance with the logic of TUIDG's geometric structures, the infinite Hexa-levels coexist: all are structured based on a given geometric dimension, all coexist in the same spatial location with six other levels from other arbitrary geometric dimensions—and these, in turn, with levels from yet other dimensions, and so on, indefinitely. In TUIDG, these infinite sets of six Structural Organization Levels coexisting in the universe are termed Coexisting Structural Organization Hexa-levels.
